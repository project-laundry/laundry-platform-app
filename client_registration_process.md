# Client registrationt process

A cleaner go in the platform and register themselves as a cleaner. When registering they need to fill in some information about themselves and the service they want to provide.

## Data we need about the cleaner

### Contact info

- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Adress

### Business & legal

- Business registration (if operating as a business vs. individual)
- Social security number or organisation number
- Bank account (if individual)

### Service to be provided

- Base address (where is the washing machine located)
- Able to pick up and deliver?
  - Operating hours (when they're available for pickup/delivery)
- Radius (How big is the area from where the pick up is available)
- Have dry tumbler
- Space to dry hang
- iron service

### Info about the waching machine

- Upload image
- Model
- Serial number
- Maximum load capacity (kg per wash cycle)
- Daily/weekly capacity (how many orders they can handle)
- Year

### Other

- Profile picture / company logo
